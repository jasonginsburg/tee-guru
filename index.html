    How to use Tee Guru

    What it is: it picks where the Blue tees go each day. Every hole has 2–3 tee pads — A is the farthest back, B is the middle, C is the most forward. The app follows the club's guidelines (the percentages and rules in Settings) so the course changes day to day without anyone doing math.

    A normal day — 30 seconds

    1. Open the link. Today's date is already picked.
    2. Tap 🎲 Generate. All 18 holes fill in.
    3. Want a hole somewhere else? Tap the letter you want on that hole. Don't like the whole thing? Generate again for a fresh deal.
    4. Tap Save. Done — everyone with the link now sees today's sheet.

    When you already know where some tees should go

    Tap the pad you want on those holes, tap the 🔒 next to each one, then hit Generate. The app builds the rest of the day around your picks. Example: pin is up on 7 and you want the tee back — set 7 to A, lock it, generate.

    Weekends

    On Friday, tap Weekend, then Generate. It makes Saturday and Sunday together — a hole won't sit in an unusual spot both days, and the 50/50 holes get one day in each spot. Flip between the two days with the Sat/Sun buttons, then Save (it saves both).

    Yellow warnings

    They mean the day breaks one of the guidelines (too many back tees, 3 and 8 matching, and so on). They're advice, not stop signs — you can always save anyway.

    History

    Every saved day is kept automatically. The season table shows where each hole has actually been versus the plan — handy proof when someone swears "18 is always back."

    Settings

    The percentages and rules live here. Tap any ? for what a rule means in plain English. Change what you like, then hit Save settings.

    Good to know

    • No accounts, no passwords — anyone with the link sees the same live sheet.
    • A saved day is locked so nobody bumps it by accident. "Edit day" reopens it.
    • Pad yardages are estimates for now, so the Blue/Hybrid totals are ballpark until real measured numbers go into Settings.
